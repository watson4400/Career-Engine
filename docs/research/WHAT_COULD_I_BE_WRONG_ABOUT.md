# WHAT COULD I BE WRONG ABOUT?

Prioritize survival over looking profitable.

1. **Fees ≠ token accrual.** Hyperliquid/Ethena/Uniswap can print fees while the liquid token underperforms for quarters. We may be buying narrative betas dressed as cash-flow disconnects.

2. **Unlock absorption is not alpha.** HYPE’s institutional unlock buyer (REPORTED) may be temporary sponsorship. Post-unlock “reconnect” can fail if the buyer distributes.

3. **Regime misread.** BTC dominance ~60% with weekly pullback can flip from `trending` to brutal alt liquidation without a clean `crisis` flag. Soft regime helpers can lag.

4. **Jev calibration drift.** Quarter-Kelly on miscalibrated probabilities still loses. Overnight Brier must actually rewrite schemas — if the operator skips gate/ship, the loop does not compound.

5. **Costs and slippage.** Synthetic paper spreads (~4 bps) understate real alt books (PENDLE, LDO). Live microstructure may zero the edge.

6. **Catalyst already priced.** NEAR +95% / 30d and Alpenglow/CCIP chatter may leave no asymmetric residue. Chasing confirmed headlines is the opposite of our mandate.

7. **Stablecoin / basis fragility.** Ethena USDe fee spikes can reverse when funding goes negative; ENA is a leveraged claim on that reflexivity.

8. **Model override creep.** Any future “let Jev set max DD” change would violate architecture. The failure mode is convenience during a drawdown.

9. **Geo / venue risk.** This build host cannot hit Binance Futures; a wrong venue adapter can poison funding/OI features.

10. **Incentive-driven TVL.** Pendle×Aave loops at 93% LTV look like product-market fit until they cascade. Sticky TVL can be leverage in costume.

**Operating rule:** If unsure whether the edge is organic, cut size to BTC beta sleeve or flat. The system that survives is the edge.
